PERSONALIZED GUIDANCE

Advice works best with a confirmed diagnosis.

Choose a crop issue or start with general prevention. Treatment suggestions should be verified for the crop, disease and local conditions.

Explore guidance

General starting points, not a diagnosis or pesticide prescription.

⌕

Check symptoms

Look at the full plant, both sides of leaves, stems and nearby plants. Note when symptoms first appeared.

☼

Reduce preventable stress

Follow crop-appropriate watering and drainage practices. Avoid unnecessary leaf wetness where relevant.

♧

Monitor the field

Record where symptoms appear and whether they spread. Keep dated photos to compare changes.

✚

Use treatments safely

Confirm the disease first. Follow the product label, protective measures, and pre-harvest intervals.

ⓘ
Important

Do not apply a pesticide based only on an unverified AI result. Confirm the diagnosis and use products registered for the specific crop and issue, following the label and local agricultural guidance.
